# Character creation and origin customization

Enable Runarcana in the world settings. The active primary GM opens the workbench from the module settings menu, selects **Character creation**, enters a name and selects an origin and class.

The three published ability methods are available: the fixed array 15/14/13/12/10/8, the 27-point purchase table (base values 8–15), and six rolls of 4d6 keeping the three highest dice. Rolled values can be reassigned between abilities; the preview verifies the allocation. A separate manual method accepts scores approved by the GM. Allocate +2 and +1 to different abilities, then choose the size, region and any required origin options. Changes invalidate the preview; create only after reviewing the current selection. Source: the collected **Character Creation** page and each origin's complete text.

Regional creation traits are available for the thirteen collected regions that publish them, including Nazumah and the Shadow Isles. Select a listed skill and the prescribed weapon/craft proficiencies. Bandle City grants two listed weapons and one listed craft; Nazumah has separate Collector and profession choices. Other regions have their own activity pools. The regional language rules supply fixed languages and any required additional choice. A language is not granted twice simply because the origin and regional page both describe it. Runeborn gains regional proficiencies only through Native and receives no regional language from that trait. Kumungu has no collected standalone creation profile; its grants remain for GM review.

Source weapon identifiers are assigned to generated weapon Items and registered with the system. Regional choices use the collected weapon categories and exact named exceptions, including nonstandard Runarcana weapons. Broad firearm choices are separated from simple and martial weapon categories. Craft categories that need a discipline or specialty require text from the player and review by the GM.

All ten origins have creation profiles. The 29 named variants are Arboreal/Floral; Nocti/Fulgor; Brutal/Envoy/Nimble; five Half-Dragon elemental essences; Aelus/Alnuk/Aurochs; Sand/Ice/Jungle Troll; eight Vastaya tribes; and Aquatic/Faeric/Fluffy Yordle. Humans use regional identity. Runeborns use their published trait gallery instead of a fixed lineage.

Permanent size, explicit movement, unconditional darkvision, skill proficiency, selected damage resistance and poison immunity use native transferable V14 effects. Technology is an Intelligence skill when Runarcana is enabled. Chosen crafts have native Tool training entries with explicit proficiency and an editable check activity. Botanical Fiber/Florescence choices retain separate feature descriptions. Vesani retain their Charisma/Wisdom choice in the character's origin plan.

Native natural-weapon Items are granted for Minotaur horns, Troll bites, the three weapons of each Half-Dragon essence, Fauhwoon horns and Kiilash claws/bites. Dice, ability selection and the published extra proficiency damage are preserved. Minotaur horns use the 19–20 threshold and recorded ×3 critical multiplier; their bonus-action attack retains its preceding-melee-attack condition. The Troll bite becomes d8 and gains the native magical property at level 6; class changes refresh its upgrade without removing other weapon properties. Runeborn Tail, Horns, Claws, Fangs and Poisonous Stinger also produce native weapon Items with the chosen damage type. The stinger's poison save still requires manual resolution.

Fluffy Yordles start with 40 feet of walking speed, falling to 30 when a heavy weapon is equipped. Equipment changes refresh this effect. The Character tab's **Underwater** control enables Marai darkvision at 60 feet and Aquatic Yordle darkvision at 120 feet; turning it off restores normal vision. Distances are explicitly stored in feet.

The workbench checks construct protection models and records the GM's confirmation of the necessary armor proficiency. Native AC formulas cover Arboreal bark, construct protection, Aurochs natural armor and the Runeborn Natural Armor trait, with an unarmored condition. Minotaur HP accumulates the per-level bonus rather than multiplying the current proficiency by every level: +1 at level 1, +12 at level 10. Aelus receive initiative advantage; Alnuk's Strength damage reduction is applied before native resistance, except for true damage. Half-Dragon resistance includes its magical variant at level 15; class changes refresh the managed effects.

Runeborn offers controlled (3 minor/2 major), moderate (4/3) and strong (6/4) budgets. Conversion is limited to one minor and one major trait. Extra minor/major/superior traits cost 1/3/6 points; published disadvantages supply their listed points. The preview checks named prerequisites, repetition rules, the darkvision cap, movement additions and attribute reductions. Traits, selected parameters and their complete instructions become separate actor features.

Runeborn Military choices grant native weapon proficiency. Specialist accepts an existing skill proficiency or a Skill trait in the same selection, regardless of row order. Skillfull checks its required skill/craft proficiency and grants advantage for the selected trained skill or craft. Craft checks use a registered native tool key so they apply the training Item's proficiency instead of becoming plain ability checks.

**Character → Customize origin** works for origins created by this workbench. The operation replaces managed origin documents and their effects, subtracts the recorded old ability boosts and adds the new ones. Ability increases made afterward are preserved. Class items and unrelated items are retained. A failed replacement restores the old origin documents and score/AC data. An origin added through the native sheet must first be removed through its native advancement workflow. Keep using the workbench to replace a managed origin; manually deleting its race item does not reverse stored ability boosts or the managed AC formula.

Origin and Region Heritage pools are tracked separately. The Character tab derives their budgets from the managed origin, including Origin Heritage gains at levels 5, 9, 13 and 17. Runeborn receives those gains only when its Heritage trait is chosen. Previously spent Region points do not consume the Origin pool. Human Inspiration and Chirean Energy have native counters, preserved when reapplying the same origin, with long-rest recovery through Runarcana. Class creation uses the published hit die and native HitPoints advancement for all nine classes.

The Heritage selector contains 207 actual description entries, with a fixed one-point cost. It checks the source's General/Origin/Region categories, recognized origin, lineage, class and region prerequisites, numeric ability requirements, repeated choices and creation-only restrictions. Colossus may be selected twice during creation; a third selection is rejected. Region points cannot buy new Heritages after creation. **Finish character creation** closes the initial-choice stage. Unresolved compound prerequisites, narrative requirements and unclassified source entries remain visible in the preview and require an explicit recorded GM override. The server recalculates costs and eligibility when applying, and rolls back newly created Items if recording the choice fails.

Thirty Heritages from the reviewed source snapshot have permanent conversions. These include the published skills for Bioenergy, Blessed Healer, Draconic Resilience, Extrovert, Nomadic Life and other descriptions; Sea Style grants net, spear and trident proficiency; Truth Seeker adds 1 to passive Perception. Arcanist Acolyte, Lunar Sight, Valor of the Protector and Perceptive retain their skill/sense effects. Higher existing proficiencies and senses are preserved. Pickpocketer repeats "proficiency" in the source and therefore does not grant invented expertise.

Eight descriptions expose their skill selection in the Character tab: Alpha Authority, Commander, Insightful Mind, Mediator, Natural Propulsion, Thick Feet, Wealthy and Scout. The first seven grant expertise if the chosen skill is already proficient; Scout instead grants advantage and half-proficiency bonus to passive Perception. Exquisite Construction requires a Brutal Construct and a second resistance from its published list, distinct from the resistance chosen for the origin. Preview and Apply validate selections against source metadata, record them on the Item and choice ledger, and reject malformed selections even with a GM eligibility override. Multiple heritage grants applied together see earlier proficiencies in that transaction. Their other abilities retain their manual instructions. A changed source hash disables reviewed conversions pending review. Previously acquired text-only Heritages are not retroactively converted by an update; repeated acquisition remains subject to the existing duplicate restrictions.

## Remaining mechanics

The complete rule descriptions remain available for conditional abilities. This release does not automate every origin's combat actions, breath weapons, Floral branch creation, spell grants, transformations, regeneration, weakness saves, situational senses, or Runeborn disadvantage effects and advanced trait interactions. Natural-weapon riders and action conditions retain their instructions. Construct DR remains recorded for review because the general armor DR examples are inconsistent. Pasts, most heritage effects, equipment and class choices need separate completion. Regional craft disciplines, the source's extra “Intuition” label in Ionia and regions without published creation traits require source review. A Tool check's default Intelligence ability is editable; source-specific craft abilities require review. Source disagreements are retained, including the general creation page's different Runeborn Heritage point count; the origin-specific profile uses its stated zero points.

Characters created with earlier workbench versions can pick **Customize origin**, complete the new regional choices and reapply their selection to receive the current weapon and training entries. Their recorded boosts are replaced, and later score gains remain intact. Older heritage records with resolvable source metadata are recalculated at their published cost; legacy unclassified entries retain their recorded cost pending review.

The source profiles are regenerated from the committed corpus. Unit tests cover the published numeric examples, selection constraints, budgets, class hit dice and replacement rollback. Foundry V14 runtime verification is still outstanding; the installed V12 world was not changed.
