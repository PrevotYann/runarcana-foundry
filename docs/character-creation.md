# Character creation and origin customization

Enable Runarcana in the world settings. The active primary GM opens the workbench from the module settings menu, selects **Character creation**, enters a name and selects an origin and class.

The three published ability methods are available: the fixed array 15/14/13/12/10/8, the 27-point purchase table (base values 8–15), and six rolls of 4d6 keeping the three highest dice. Rolled values can be reassigned between abilities; the preview verifies the allocation. A separate manual method accepts scores approved by the GM. Allocate +2 and +1 to different abilities, then choose the size, region and any required origin options. Changes invalidate the preview; create only after reviewing the current selection. Source: the collected **Character Creation** page and each origin's complete text.

Regional creation traits are available for the thirteen collected regions that publish them, including Nazumah and the Shadow Isles. Select a listed skill and the prescribed weapon/craft proficiencies. Bandle City grants two listed weapons and one listed craft; Nazumah has separate Collector and profession choices. Other regions have their own activity pools. The regional language rules supply fixed languages and any required additional choice. A language is not granted twice simply because the origin and regional page both describe it. Runeborn gains regional proficiencies only through Native and receives no regional language from that trait. Kumungu has no collected standalone creation profile; its grants remain for GM review.

Source weapon identifiers are assigned to generated weapon Items and registered with the system. Regional choices use the collected weapon categories and exact named exceptions, including nonstandard Runarcana weapons. Broad firearm choices are separated from simple and martial weapon categories. Craft categories that need a discipline or specialty require text from the player and review by the GM.

All ten origins have creation profiles. The 29 named variants are Arboreal/Floral; Nocti/Fulgor; Brutal/Envoy/Nimble; five Half-Dragon elemental essences; Aelus/Alnuk/Aurochs; Sand/Ice/Jungle Troll; eight Vastaya tribes; and Aquatic/Faeric/Fluffy Yordle. Humans use regional identity. Runeborns use their published trait gallery instead of a fixed lineage.

Permanent size, explicit movement, unconditional darkvision, skill proficiency, selected damage resistance and poison immunity use native transferable V14 effects. Technology is an Intelligence skill when Runarcana is enabled. Chosen crafts have native Tool training entries with the published half-proficiency multiplier and an editable check activity. Recognized craft names use their published ability; unclassified names retain an editable Intelligence default and a review flag. Botanical Fiber/Florescence choices retain separate feature descriptions. Vesani retain their Charisma/Wisdom choice in the character's origin plan.

Native natural-weapon Items are granted for Minotaur horns, Troll bites, the three weapons of each Half-Dragon essence, Fauhwoon horns and Kiilash claws/bites. Dice, ability selection and the published extra proficiency damage are preserved. Minotaur horns use the 19–20 threshold and recorded ×3 critical multiplier; their bonus-action attack retains its preceding-melee-attack condition. The Troll bite becomes d8 and gains the native magical property at level 6; class changes refresh its upgrade without removing other weapon properties. Runeborn Tail, Horns, Claws, Fangs and Poisonous Stinger also produce native weapon Items with the chosen damage type. The stinger's poison save still requires manual resolution.

Fluffy Yordles start with 40 feet of walking speed, falling to 30 when a heavy weapon is equipped. Equipment changes refresh this effect. The Character tab's **Underwater** control enables Marai darkvision at 60 feet and Aquatic Yordle darkvision at 120 feet; turning it off restores normal vision. Distances are explicitly stored in feet.

The workbench checks construct protection models and records the GM's confirmation of the necessary armor proficiency. Native AC formulas cover Arboreal bark, construct protection, Aurochs natural armor and the Runeborn Natural Armor trait, with an unarmored condition. Minotaur HP accumulates the per-level bonus rather than multiplying the current proficiency by every level: +1 at level 1, +12 at level 10. Aelus receive initiative advantage; Alnuk's Strength damage reduction is applied before native resistance, except for true damage. Half-Dragon resistance includes its magical variant at level 15; class changes refresh the managed effects.

Runeborn offers controlled (3 minor/2 major), moderate (4/3) and strong (6/4) budgets. Conversion is limited to one minor and one major trait. Extra minor/major/superior traits cost 1/3/6 points; published disadvantages supply their listed points. The preview checks named prerequisites, repetition rules, the darkvision cap, movement additions and attribute reductions. Traits, selected parameters and their complete instructions become separate actor features.

Runeborn Military choices grant native weapon proficiency. Specialist accepts an existing skill proficiency or a Skill trait in the same selection, regardless of row order. Skillfull checks its required skill/craft proficiency and grants advantage for the selected trained skill or craft. Craft checks use a registered native tool key so they apply the training Item's proficiency instead of becoming plain ability checks.

**Character → Customize origin** works for origins created by this workbench. The operation replaces managed origin documents and their effects, subtracts the recorded old ability boosts and adds the new ones. Ability increases made afterward are preserved. Class items and unrelated items are retained. A failed replacement restores the old origin documents and score/AC data. An origin added through the native sheet must first be removed through its native advancement workflow. Keep using the workbench to replace a managed origin; manually deleting its race item does not reverse stored ability boosts or the managed AC formula.

Origin and Region Heritage pools are tracked separately. The Character tab derives their budgets from the managed origin, including Origin Heritage gains at levels 5, 9, 13 and 17. Runeborn receives those gains only when its Heritage trait is chosen. Previously spent Region points do not consume the Origin pool. Human Inspiration and Chirean Energy have native counters, preserved when reapplying the same origin, with long-rest recovery through Runarcana. Class creation uses the published hit die and native HitPoints advancement for all nine classes.

The Heritage selector contains 207 actual description entries, with a fixed one-point cost. It checks the source's General/Origin/Region categories, recognized origin, lineage, class and region prerequisites, numeric ability requirements, repeated choices and creation-only restrictions. Colossus may be selected twice during creation; a third selection is rejected. Region points cannot buy new Heritages after creation. **Finish character creation** closes the initial-choice stage. Unresolved compound prerequisites, narrative requirements and unclassified source entries remain visible in the preview and require an explicit recorded GM override. The server recalculates costs and eligibility when applying, and rolls back newly created Items if recording the choice fails.

Forty-three Heritages from the reviewed source snapshot have permanent conversions. These include the published skills for Bioenergy, Blessed Healer, Draconic Resilience, Extrovert, Nomadic Life and other descriptions; Sea Style grants net, spear and trident proficiency; Truth Seeker adds 1 to passive Perception. Arcanist Acolyte, Lunar Sight, Valor of the Protector and Perceptive retain their skill/sense effects. Higher existing proficiencies and senses are preserved. Pickpocketer repeats "proficiency" in the source and therefore does not grant invented expertise.

Eight descriptions expose their skill selection in the Character tab: Alpha Authority, Commander, Insightful Mind, Mediator, Natural Propulsion, Thick Feet, Wealthy and Scout. The first seven grant expertise if the chosen skill is already proficient; Scout instead grants advantage and half-proficiency bonus to passive Perception. Exquisite Construction requires a Brutal Construct and a second resistance from its published list, distinct from the resistance chosen for the origin. Preview and Apply validate selections against source metadata, record them on the Item and choice ledger, and reject malformed selections even with a GM eligibility override. Multiple heritage grants applied together see earlier proficiencies in that transaction. Their other abilities retain their manual instructions. A changed source hash disables reviewed conversions pending review. Previously acquired text-only Heritages are not retroactively converted by an update; repeated acquisition remains subject to the existing duplicate restrictions.

Skilled now requires three distinct new proficiencies, each chosen from the skill list or the collected craft catalog. Master Artisan chooses a craft and receives expertise: the normal half-proficiency contribution becomes a full bonus. Already expert crafts receive the source's additional +1 instead of doubling again. Hospitable grants Cook training, and Cautious Traveler requires a Guide discipline in addition to its Survival proficiency/expertise. The catalog contains 41 published crafts/disciplines and their check attributes. Guide fields require a map type, route or vehicle; Cards requires the chosen game details. Newly granted training entries and skill effects are created in one transaction and removed together if recording fails. Each craft has its own proficiency multiplier; expertise with one does not improve unrelated crafts.

Craft grants create a separate training Tool with a check Activity; they do not change existing inventory Tools or grant physical craft sets. Use the new training entry for the granted proficiency or expertise. Craft levels, formulas, daily production, conditional natural-20 effects and spell grants still need separate implementation. Existing origin training entries created before this version can be corrected by reapplying **Customize origin**; their old explicit multiplier is not silently changed by updating the module.

Weapon selections now cover Discipline is Strength (any catalog weapon), Ferromancer (martial weapons, excluding firearms and weapons exotic in the chosen region), Weapon Scholar (weapons marked regional in the source table, including weapons of other regions), and Sulfur, Saltpeter and Charcoal (firearms). The firearm prerequisite is checked using explicit weapon proficiency identifiers or a weapon Item's explicit proficiency; a generic martial proficiency does not satisfy it. Each choice is recorded and grants the native weapon proficiency identifier. The complete source instructions retain spell mysteries, mana discounts, formulas, craft-level bonuses and misfire actions that are not automated here.

Discipline is Strength permits repeated acquisitions at one point each, with a distinct weapon each time. Its first two acquisitions grant the published proficiency-bonus damage benefit; later acquisitions grant proficiency without another damage bonus. Owned granted heritage Items control the damage benefit, which follows the actor's current proficiency bonus and applies once to the first component of a native weapon Attack damage roll. Duplicate selections and repeated processing of the same configuration do not stack the bonus. Deleting a granted heritage Item removes its damage benefit; its recorded point expenditure remains in the choice ledger. Existing text-only acquisitions are not retroactively converted. Native critical-modifier settings still govern numeric bonuses.

The explicit repeat permission for Sho'Ma Form is retained, but acquiring a different tribal form and applying transformations still requires recorded GM review. Colossus keeps its two-acquisition limit. Regional creation menus reject weapons that become exotic outside their published region. Ranged simple weapons now use the correct native simpleR category. Native sheet combat proficiency for exotic weapons obtained outside the workbench, and Advanced Weapons/improper exceptions, still require review.

The language catalog retains the chapter's 13 Common, 14 Exotic and 7 Dead language entries. Oovi-Kat now selects exactly one Exotic language or two distinct Common languages; Dead languages and invented entries cannot fill that choice. Languages already granted by its origin/regional selection cannot be counted again. Extrovert requires one additional Common language and records its choice alongside the History proficiency/expertise. Its language effect uses a semicolon-separated custom entry. Existing actor languages are checked for duplicates, including published regional spelling/dialect equivalents such as Piltovan/Zaunite and Minotaur/Minotauric. Campaign language approaches and additional dialect rulings remain with the GM. Older Oovi-Kat choices that contain only one Common language must be corrected when reapplying Customize origin.

Mountain's Shepherd offers Apothecary or Guide: Pilot with the published terrestrial examples Rickshaw, Cart, Wagon, Carriage, Horse, Motorbike and Car. It also applies Animal Handling proficiency/expertise. These vehicle examples are not an exhaustive catalog; other land vehicles require manual GM completion. The animal worth up to 100 gp remains a manual grant. Prodigy Musician grants Artist: Musician training, or individual craft expertise if the character already has that craft; an already expert craft receives the published additional +1. Its Sound cantrip, daily uses and song DC modifiers remain manual. Updating the module does not retroactively replace acquired world Heritage Items.

Four additional reviewed permanent conversions are available. Amphibious Constitution grants a minimum 35-foot swim speed, preserving a faster existing swim speed; its breathing, terrain and healing instructions remain manual. Vastayan Blood gives advantage on Medicine checks without inventing Medicine proficiency. Primal Fighter selects Intimidation, Animal Handling, Nature or Survival and grants proficiency/expertise; its conditional unarmored AC and first-hit damage remain manual. Graciousness grants Charisma saving proficiency, or expertise when the character already has proficiency. Expertise uses an additional current-proficiency roll bonus while keeping the native proficiency field within its permitted 0?1 range. Existing expertise from owned managed effects and earlier choices in the same transaction is recognized, so recorded GM repeat overrides do not stack that bonus. Its bonus-action Dodge remains manual.

Reviewed origin actions now appear in the creation preview and are granted as separate managed native feature Items. All five Half-Dragon essences grant a base-form breath action: cold/fire/thunder in a 15-foot cone, lightning/acid in a 20-foot by 5-foot line. The activity unlocks at character level 2 and uses a Dexterity save against 8 + Constitution modifier + proficiency bonus, dealing proficiency bonus d6 damage with half damage on success. Its maximum uses follow current proficiency and are consumed by the native workflow. Only the Runarcana full rest restores them; short and long rests do not. Reapplying or changing the essence preserves spent breath uses, and failed replacement restores the prior Item. Draconic Form's extra uses, die changes and essence-specific effects remain manual; this activity represents the base form.

All eight Vastaya tribes grant a native Transformation check for minor appearance changes: bonus action, Constitution check with an additional proficiency bonus, DC 15, targeting the character itself. The activity does not change tribe, size or statistics. Magic-zone restrictions, advantage/disadvantage, exhaustion, regeneration and rerolls remain manual. Reviewed action profiles are tied to the source-page hashes; a changed source disables their regeneration pending review. Existing characters receive the new actions by reapplying Customize origin; module updates do not silently rewrite owned Items.

## Remaining mechanics

The complete rule descriptions remain available for conditional abilities. This release does not automate every origin's combat actions, transformed breath weapons, Floral branch creation, spell grants, transformations, regeneration, weakness saves, situational senses, or Runeborn disadvantage effects and advanced trait interactions. Natural-weapon riders and action conditions retain their instructions. Construct DR remains recorded for review because the general armor DR examples are inconsistent. Pasts, most heritage effects, equipment and class choices need separate completion. Regional craft disciplines, the source's extra “Intuition” label in Ionia and regions without published creation traits require source review. A Tool check's ability is editable; unclassified craft names and formula-specific ability changes require review. Source disagreements are retained, including the general creation page's different Runeborn Heritage point count; the origin-specific profile uses its stated zero points.

Characters created with earlier workbench versions can pick **Customize origin**, complete the new regional choices and reapply their selection to receive the current weapon and training entries. Their recorded boosts are replaced, and later score gains remain intact. Older heritage records with resolvable source metadata are recalculated at their published cost; legacy unclassified entries retain their recorded cost pending review.

The source profiles are regenerated from the committed corpus. Unit tests cover the published numeric examples, selection constraints, budgets, class hit dice and replacement rollback. Foundry V14 runtime verification is still outstanding; the installed V12 world was not changed.
